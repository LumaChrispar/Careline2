Design a distinctive, beautifully balanced rounded lowercase c formed by two interlocking continuous ribbon shapes that suggest people supporting one another and continuity of care, with a small warm terracotta node at the open upper-right end. The negative space should feel open and welcoming. Bold, simple silhouette readable at 24 pixels. Flat, crisp, vector-friendly geometry with subtle optical refinements, premium contemporary identity. Deep forest green #176b59 as primary, warm terracotta #C77851 as a small accent. Genuine transparent background. Square composition, symbol centred and occupying roughly 75 percent of the canvas, generous equal margins. No gradients, shadows, textures, mockups, letters, captions, crosses, ECG lines, flags or decorative extras. Output a high resolution transparent PNG.
